CACI facials use gentle microcurrent to tone the muscles of the face, with options for the whole face, the eyes and the jowls, and microdermabrasion for a smoother surface.

### Page copy

#### What it is

CACI is a non-surgical facial that uses low-level microcurrent, delivered through small probes, to stimulate the muscles of the face. It involves no needles and no cuts. Many clients choose it because they would like their skin to look and feel more toned and refreshed.

Our menu has options for different areas. The CACI Non-Surgical Face Lift (the treatment’s menu name) works on the whole face, the CACI Non-Surgical Eye Lift (also a menu name) focuses on the eye area, and the CACI Jowl Lift Express Facial works on the lower face and jawline. The Ultimate Anti-Ageing Facial is our longest CACI option and combines several steps in one session.

CACI microdermabrasion is a separate, gentler option. It uses a fine exfoliating tip to lift away dead skin cells from the surface, which can leave skin looking brighter and feeling smoother. You can have it on its own or with a hydrating mask.

#### Who it suits

CACI suits adults who would like to look after their skin, or who are noticing changes in firmness and tone. Microdermabrasion suits many skin types, especially if your skin feels dull or rough.

It is not suitable for everyone. Microcurrent is typically avoided if you have a pacemaker or another implanted electrical device, epilepsy, are pregnant, or have metal implants in the treatment area. Your therapist will also ask about recent injectable treatments, skin infections and any medication. Please tell us about anything that applies, even if it seems small.

#### What to expect

1. **Before your treatment.** Your therapist asks about your skin, health and medication, looks at your skin and talks through which CACI option suits you. Please arrive with clean skin if you can.
2. **During your treatment.** Your skin is cleansed, then the therapist moves the probes in set patterns over the area. You will usually feel a light tingling. Microdermabrasion feels like a gentle, slightly scratchy massage.
3. **Afterwards.** Your skin may look a little pink for a short time. Most people carry on with their day straight away.

Results typically build over a course, and top-up sessions help keep them going.

#### Aftercare

Use a gentle moisturiser and a daily sun protection product, especially after microdermabrasion, as freshly exfoliated skin is more sensitive to the sun. Keep your skin routine simple for the rest of the day and avoid harsh exfoliating products for a few days, unless your therapist tells you otherwise. If your skin feels sore or looks unusually red, contact the salon. Our safety and aftercare page will have more advice once it is published.

### Questions and answers

**What does CACI feel like?**

Most clients describe a gentle tingling or light pulsing as the therapist moves small probes over the skin. It should not be painful, and you can ask the therapist to adjust the level at any point.

**Do I need a course of CACI?**

CACI is typically recommended as a course of sessions, with the number depending on your skin and what you would like to change. Your therapist will suggest a plan at your appointment. We’ll talk you through the number of sessions and the cost before you start.

**Is CACI the same as microneedling?**

No. CACI uses microcurrent applied to the surface of the skin, with no needles. Microneedling is a different treatment that makes tiny controlled channels in the skin.

**Who should not have CACI?**

Microcurrent is not suitable for everyone, for example if you have a pacemaker or other implanted electrical device, epilepsy, or are pregnant. Your therapist will go through a full list with you before you begin.

**Can I wear make-up afterwards?**

Your therapist will advise when you can wear make-up again, usually after a short break to let your skin settle.

### Introduction

Dermaplaning gently removes dead skin cells and fine facial hair for a smoother surface. Have it on its own as a luxury facial, or with a glycolic peel.

### Page copy

#### What it is

Dermaplaning is a manual exfoliation. Your therapist uses a sterile, single-use surgical blade, held at an angle and moved in light strokes across the skin, to lift away dead skin cells and the fine, pale hair on the face. This is sometimes called peach fuzz.

Taking away that top layer can leave skin looking brighter and feeling smoother, and make skincare and make-up sit more evenly afterwards. It is not a medical treatment and does not promise a particular result.

We offer two options. Dermaplaning Luxury is a full facial built around the dermaplaning step. Dermaplaning and Glycolic Peel adds a glycolic acid peel for further exfoliation in the same appointment.

#### Who it suits

Dermaplaning suits adults who would like smoother skin, who find their make-up sits unevenly, or who would like to remove fine facial hair without waxing or threading. It works with most skin types.

It is not suitable if you have active acne or inflamed spots, a skin infection, cold sores, sunburn or broken skin in the area. Your therapist will also ask about medication, recent skin treatments and any skin conditions. If you are pregnant, tell your therapist before you book. Dermaplaning removes fine, light hair, so it is not suited to thick or dark hair.

#### What to expect

1. **Before your treatment.** Your therapist asks about your skin and health, then checks your skin and explains the steps. Please arrive without make-up if you can, or it will be cleansed off at the start.
2. **During your treatment.** Your skin is cleansed, then the therapist glides the blade across your face in short, light strokes. The rest of the facial follows, including the glycolic peel if you have chosen it.
3. **Afterwards.** Your skin will feel soft and smooth. It may look slightly pink or feel a little sensitive for a short time.

#### Aftercare

Use a gentle moisturiser and a daily sun protection product, as freshly exfoliated skin is more sensitive to the sun. For a few days, avoid strong exfoliating products, retinol or active skincare, and hot treatments such as saunas, unless your therapist advises otherwise. Do not pick or scrub the skin. If it feels sore or looks unusually red, contact the salon. Our safety and aftercare page will have more advice once it is published.

### Questions and answers

**Does dermaplaning hurt?**

Most people find it comfortable. The blade is held at an angle and moved in light, short strokes, which can feel like a soft scraping or tickling sensation.

**Will the hair grow back thicker?**

No. Dermaplaning removes fine, light hair at the surface and does not change the way it grows. It typically grows back with the same texture as before.

**How often can I have dermaplaning?**

Many people have it every three to four weeks, in line with the skin's natural renewal cycle. Your therapist will advise what suits your skin.

**Can I wear make-up afterwards?**

Your therapist will advise when you can wear make-up again, usually after a short break, as freshly exfoliated skin can be more sensitive.

**Should I choose dermaplaning on its own or with a glycolic peel?**

Dermaplaning Luxury is a good place to start. Adding a glycolic peel gives a deeper exfoliation as well, so it suits skin that feels dull or uneven. Your therapist will recommend one after looking at your skin.

### Introduction

A glycolic peel uses a gentle acid to lift away dead skin cells, leaving skin looking fresher and feeling smoother. It is also available with dermaplaning.

### Page copy

#### What it is

Glycolic acid is an alpha hydroxy acid. In a glycolic peel, your therapist applies it to the skin for a short, timed period. It loosens the bonds between dead skin cells so they can be removed from the surface. This can leave the skin looking brighter, feeling smoother and more even in tone.

Our Glycolic Peel Facial typically includes a cleanse, the peel itself, then soothing and protecting products. The strength and timing are chosen to suit your skin.

If you would like dermaplaning as well, the combined Dermaplaning and Glycolic Peel treatment is on our dermaplaning page.

#### Who it suits

A glycolic peel suits adults whose skin looks dull, uneven or rough, or who would like a regular exfoliating treatment. Your therapist will check your skin first and tell you honestly whether a peel is right for you.

It is not suitable if your skin is sunburnt, broken, inflamed or infected, or if you have a cold sore. Please tell your therapist if you are pregnant, are breastfeeding, take certain medications, have used strong skincare actives recently, or have reacted badly to acids before. Your therapist may suggest a patch test or a gentler option if your skin is sensitive.

#### What to expect

1. **Before your treatment.** Your therapist asks about your skin, health and routine, checks your skin and explains the peel.
2. **During your treatment.** Your skin is cleansed, then the peel is applied for a timed period. You will feel warmth or tingling. The peel is then removed or neutralised and your skin is soothed and protected.
3. **Afterwards.** Your skin may look pink and feel tight or warm. Mild flaking can follow over the next few days.

Results typically last a few weeks, and a course builds on them.

#### Aftercare

Sun protection is essential, as newly peeled skin is more sensitive to the sun. Apply a daily sun protection product and avoid strong sun for a few days. Moisturise gently and avoid exfoliating products, retinol and other active skincare, as well as waxing, saunas and hot baths, until your skin has settled or your therapist says otherwise. Do not pick flaking skin. If your skin feels sore, blisters or looks unusually red, contact the salon. Our safety and aftercare page will have more advice once it is published.

### Questions and answers

**Does a glycolic peel hurt?**

You will typically feel a warm tingling for a minute or two while the peel is on your skin. Your therapist watches your skin closely and removes the peel when it has done its job. Tell them straight away if it becomes uncomfortable.

**Will my skin peel?**

It may, though many people see slight dryness or flaking. Do not pick at it, and let any flaking come away by itself.

**How many peels do I need?**

One peel can leave skin looking fresher, and many people have a series of peels spaced a few weeks apart. Your therapist will suggest a plan that suits your skin.

**Can I have a peel if I use retinol?**

Tell your therapist what you use. You may be asked to pause retinol or other active products for a few days before your peel. Your therapist will tell you exactly what to pause and for how long.

**Can I combine a peel with dermaplaning?**

Yes. Dermaplaning and Glycolic Peel is a combined treatment on our dermaplaning page.
