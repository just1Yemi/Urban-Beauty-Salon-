### Page copy

#### What it is

Dermal fillers are soft gels, most often made from hyaluronic acid, a substance found naturally in the skin. Placed carefully beneath the skin, they add volume, shape or support where it has been lost or where you would like a little more definition.

Our menu covers lip filler, chin and mid-face enhancement, jawline definition, smile lines (nasolabial folds), marionette lines, upper lip lines and the tear trough area under the eyes. ### Page copy

#### What it is

Skin boosters are treatments that place small amounts of hyaluronic acid, a substance found naturally in the skin, within the skin itself. Rather than adding volume to one area, they are designed to improve hydration, texture and the overall quality of the skin.

We offer several skin boosters: Profhilo, Jalupro Super Hydro, Seventy Hyal, Juvederm Volite, RRS Long Lasting and RRS Hyalift. They differ in their ingredients, how they spread within the skin and how many sessions are usually advised. Your practitioner will recommend the one that suits your skin and explain why.

#### Who it suits

Skin boosters can suit adults whose skin feels dry, dull or less firm than it used to, or who would like to improve skin texture on the face, neck, décolletage or hands. They are often chosen by people who want fresher-looking skin without a change in shape.

Treatment is not usually suitable if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have an active skin infection in the area or have certain medical conditions. Please tell us about your health and any medicines when you book. Treatments are for adults aged 18 and over.

#### What to expect

1. **Before your treatment.** You start with a consultation about your skin, your health, what you would like to improve, the options, the risks and the cost. Your skin is cleansed before treatment.
2. **During your treatment.** Your practitioner makes a series of small injections with a fine needle. Most appointments take around 45 minutes, including time to talk.
3. **Afterwards.** Small raised bumps where the product was placed are common and typically settle within a day or two. Some redness or bruising is possible.

After a course, results typically last around 6 months.

#### Aftercare

- avoid touching or massaging the treated areas
- avoid make-up on the area for the rest of the day
- avoid alcohol, strenuous exercise, saunas, steam rooms and sunbeds
- protect your skin from the sun with a high-factor sunscreen

Fillers add volume or shape to a particular area. Skin boosters spread a thin layer of hyaluronic acid within the skin to improve hydration and texture, so they are not designed to change the shape of your face.

**How many sessions will I need?**

Most skin boosters are given as a short course, typically two or three sessions a few weeks apart, followed by maintenance sessions. Your practitioner will recommend a plan for your skin.

**How much does Profhilo cost?**

### Introduction

Biostimulators are injectable treatments designed to encourage your skin to make more of its own collagen, for a gradual change that builds over weeks rather than an instant one.

### Page copy

#### What it is

Collagen is the protein that gives skin its firmness and structure, and the body makes less of it as we age. This is one reason skin can look thinner or less supported over time. Biostimulators are designed to work with your skin’s own repair processes. Rather than simply filling a line, they are designed to prompt the skin to produce new collagen in the treated area.

We offer two biostimulators.

### Sculptra (PLLA)

Sculptra contains poly-L-lactic acid, a material the body gradually breaks down. It is used to restore lost volume and support skin quality across broader areas, such as the temples, cheeks and jawline. The change develops slowly, so a course of treatments is typically recommended.

### Radiesse (CaHA)

Radiesse contains tiny calcium hydroxylapatite microspheres in a gel. It gives some lift straight away, then continues to stimulate collagen as the gel is absorbed. It can add structure, for example along the jawline, or be diluted and spread more thinly to support skin firmness.

Neither product is used in the lips. At your consultation, your practitioner explains which product, if either, suits your face and your goals.

#### Who it suits

Biostimulators can suit adults who have noticed a gradual loss of firmness or volume and would prefer a subtle change that builds over time. They are a longer-term option, so they suit people who are happy to wait weeks rather than see the full effect on the day.

They are not suitable if you are under 18, pregnant or breastfeeding, or have a skin infection or cold sore in the area. Some medical conditions, including certain autoimmune conditions and a tendency to form raised scars, and some medicines may also mean treatment is not right for you. Your practitioner goes through your medical history in full before treating you.

#### What to expect

1. **Before your treatment.** Your practitioner asks about your health, medicines and goals, looks at your skin and explains the options, the likely number of sessions, possible side effects and the cost.
2. **During your treatment.** The skin is cleansed and the product is placed with a fine needle or a blunt cannula. Local anaesthetic is typically used to keep you comfortable.
3. **Afterwards.** Some swelling, redness, tenderness or bruising is common and typically settles within a few days. Most people return to everyday activities straight away.
4. **Your course.** If more than one session is planned, they are typically four to six weeks apart, so your practitioner can see how your skin responds.

#### Aftercare

- Avoid touching, pressing or wearing make-up on the treated area for the rest of the day.
- Avoid strenuous exercise, saunas, steam rooms and alcohol for 24 hours.
- After Sculptra, you are typically asked to massage the area for five minutes, five times a day, for five days. Your practitioner shows you how.
- Wear a high-factor sunscreen and avoid other facial treatments for about two weeks unless your practitioner advises otherwise.
- If you notice any change to your vision, call 999 or go to A&E straight away, then let us know.
- Contact us straight away if you notice pain that is getting worse, a pale, dusky or blotchy patch of skin, or signs of infection such as heat, spreading redness or fever. [[TODO: aftercare contact hours and the urgent out-of-hours route, from Yemi]]

### Questions and answers

**Is a biostimulator the same as dermal filler?**

Not quite. Hyaluronic acid filler adds volume where it is placed. Biostimulators are designed to prompt your skin to make new collagen, so the change is more gradual. Radiesse also gives some lift on the day. Unlike hyaluronic acid filler, neither product can be dissolved once placed, which is one reason a thorough consultation matters.

**How many sessions will I need?**

It depends on the product and the area. Sculptra is typically given as a course of two or three sessions, about four to six weeks apart. Radiesse is often a single session, sometimes with a top-up. Your practitioner confirms the plan and the total cost before you start.

**When will I see a difference?**

Radiesse gives some visible support straight away. With Sculptra, early fullness from the water it is mixed with fades within a few days, and the change from new collagen typically builds over two to three months.

**Does it hurt?**

Most people describe a pinching or pressure feeling rather than pain. Local anaesthetic is typically mixed with the product or used alongside it to keep you comfortable.

**How do I book?**

Book online at any time with any Book button, or call the salon during opening hours. Your appointment starts with a consultation, and there is no pressure to go ahead on the day.

### Introduction

Microneedling uses very fine needles to create tiny channels in the skin, prompting its natural repair process. It is a gradual, course-based treatment for texture, scarring, pigmentation and glow.

### Page copy

#### What it is

Microneedling, sometimes called collagen induction therapy, uses a small handheld device fitted with very fine, sterile needles. The needles create thousands of tiny channels in the top layers of the skin. The skin responds as it would to any small injury, by starting its natural repair process, which includes making new collagen and elastin.

Because the change comes from the skin’s own repair, it builds gradually over the weeks after each session. The tiny channels also help serums applied during the treatment to reach deeper into the skin.

We offer three microneedling treatments, each tailored to a different concern:

- **Hydration and glow:** for skin that looks dull, tired or dehydrated.
- **Acne scars:** for softening the look of some types of acne scarring and uneven texture.
- **Pigmentation:** for uneven skin tone and patches of darker skin.

Your practitioner adjusts the needle depth and the serum used to suit your skin and the concern being treated.

#### Who it suits

Microneedling can suit adults with uneven texture, enlarged-looking pores, fine lines, dullness, some acne scarring or uneven pigmentation. It is a good fit if you prefer a gradual change and are happy to commit to a course.

It is not suitable if you have active acne breakouts, a cold sore or skin infection in the area, a flare of eczema, psoriasis or rosacea, or a tendency to form raised (keloid) scars. You should also not have it if you are pregnant, have sunburn, or have taken isotretinoin for acne in the last six months. Some blood disorders and blood-thinning medicines may also mean it is not right for you. Your practitioner checks your medical history before treating you.

#### What to expect

1. **Before your treatment.** Your practitioner assesses your skin and talks through your goals. You are typically asked to stop retinoids and exfoliating acids for three to five days beforehand and to avoid tanning.
2. **During your treatment.** Your skin is cleansed and a numbing cream is applied. The device is passed gently over the skin in sections, and a serum is applied to finish.
3. **Afterwards.** Your skin will look pink or red and may feel warm and tight, like mild sunburn. This typically settles within one to three days, and some light flaking may follow.
4. **Your course.** Sessions are typically four to six weeks apart, giving your skin time to repair between treatments.

Results typically last several months, with maintenance sessions to keep them going.

#### Aftercare

- Avoid make-up for 24 hours, and keep to a gentle cleanser and moisturiser for the next few days.
- Avoid retinoids, exfoliating acids and scrubs for at least three to five days, or as your practitioner advises.
- Wear SPF 30 or higher every day and avoid direct sun while your skin recovers.
- Avoid exercise, swimming pools, saunas and steam rooms for 24 to 48 hours.
- Let any flaking come away naturally; do not pick or peel the skin.
- Contact us if you notice spreading redness, heat, swelling or other signs of infection. [[TODO: aftercare contact hours and the out-of-hours route, from Yemi]]

### Questions and answers

**How much does microneedling cost?**

Price on consultation. Your practitioner confirms the cost of each session, and of any course, before you start.

**Does it hurt?**

Most people describe a light scratching or vibrating feeling. A numbing cream is typically applied beforehand to keep you comfortable.

**How many sessions will I need?**

Most people have a course, typically three to six sessions about four to six weeks apart. Acne scarring and pigmentation often need more sessions than a hydration and glow treatment. Your practitioner suggests a plan at your consultation.

**Is microneedling suitable for darker skin tones?**

Microneedling is typically suitable for a wide range of skin tones, as it does not rely on heat or light. Darker skin can be more prone to changes in pigmentation after any skin treatment, so your practitioner assesses your skin carefully and adjusts the treatment.

**Can I have microneedling alongside other facials?**

Yes, it can be planned alongside facials such as CACI or COMCIT, with suitable gaps between treatments. Your practitioner advises on timing so your skin has time to recover.

### Introduction

Scalp treatments, including PRP and Plinest Hair, for people concerned about thinning hair. Every plan starts with a consultation, because hair loss has many causes and many types do not respond to treatment.

### Page copy

#### What it is

Hair loss treatments aim to support the scalp and the hair follicles that are still active. They are a course of treatments, and changes take months to judge because hair grows slowly. We offer three options, and your practitioner helps you decide which, if any, is right for you.

### PRP (platelet-rich plasma)

A small sample of your blood is taken and spun in a machine called a centrifuge to concentrate the platelets. This platelet-rich plasma is then injected into the thinning areas of the scalp. Platelets contain growth factors involved in the body’s natural repair, and PRP is used with the aim of supporting hair follicles, but the evidence is still developing and results vary, so some people see little or no change. PRP is available as a single session or as a course of three.

### Plinest Hair

Plinest Hair is a polynucleotide treatment. Polynucleotides are purified DNA fragments, derived from fish, that are injected into the scalp to support the condition of the skin around the hair follicles.

### Hair filler

Hair filler is a scalp injection treatment. [[TODO: confirm the hair filler product, what it contains and how it works, for the practitioner to describe accurately]]

#### Who it suits

These treatments may be discussed if you have early thinning and your practitioner finds active hair follicles. They are not designed for completely bald areas or scarring types of hair loss.

Hair loss can be linked to iron levels, thyroid problems, hormones, stress or other health conditions. If your hair loss is sudden, patchy or rapid, or you have other symptoms, please see your GP first.

Treatment is not suitable if you are under 18, pregnant or breastfeeding, or have a scalp infection. PRP is not suitable if you have a blood or platelet disorder or take blood-thinning medicines, and Plinest Hair is not suitable if you have a fish allergy. Your practitioner goes through your medical history in full before treating you.

#### What to expect

1. **Your consultation.** Your practitioner asks about your hair loss, health and medicines, looks closely at your scalp and talks through realistic expectations, the likely number of sessions and the cost.
2. **During your treatment.** Your scalp is cleansed. For PRP, a blood sample is taken and prepared during your appointment. The treatment is then given as a series of small injections across the thinning area.
3. **Afterwards.** Your scalp may feel tender, with small pinpoint marks or mild swelling. This typically settles within one to two days, and most people return to everyday activities straight away.
4. **Your course.** Sessions are typically four to six weeks apart, followed by maintenance sessions. Your practitioner reviews your progress over the following months.

Results vary from person to person. Once your course is complete, maintenance sessions are typically every 6 to 12 months.

#### Aftercare

- Avoid washing your hair until the next day, then use a gentle shampoo.
- Avoid hair colouring, perming and other chemical treatments for 48 to 72 hours.
- Avoid strenuous exercise, saunas, steam rooms and swimming pools for 24 hours.
- Ask your practitioner whether to pause any medicines or supplements around your treatment, and do not stop a prescribed medicine without advice.

### Questions and answers

**Do I need a consultation?**

Yes. Your practitioner needs to understand the type and likely cause of your hair loss before suggesting any treatment. If your hair loss is sudden, patchy or comes with other symptoms, we will suggest you see your GP before going ahead.

**When will I see a difference?**

Hair grows slowly, so changes are typically judged over three to six months rather than weeks. Results vary from person to person, and your practitioner talks through realistic expectations at your consultation.

**How many sessions will I need?**

Most people have a course, typically three or four sessions about four to six weeks apart, followed by maintenance sessions. PRP is available as a single session or a course of three.

**Does it hurt?**

Most people feel a series of small pricks across the scalp, and the scalp may feel tender for a day or two afterwards. Your practitioner talks you through ways to keep you comfortable.

**Are these treatments suitable for both men and women?**

Yes. Thinning hair affects men and women, and the treatments can suit both. Your practitioner tailors the plan to your hair and scalp.
