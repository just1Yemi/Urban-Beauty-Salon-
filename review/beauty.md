#### What it is

Our lash and brow menu covers the treatments most people ask for. A lash lift or LVL (Length, Volume and Lift) curls and lifts your own lashes from the root, so they appear longer and more open. A lash or brow tint adds colour to fine or fair hairs. Brow lamination sets your brow hairs in a brushed-up, fuller-looking shape. You can also have the Korean lash lift or LVL together with an eyebrow wax and tint, or LVL and brow lamination as one package.

If you would like extra length and fullness, lash extensions are available as an express set, with infills to keep them looking neat and a removal when you are ready for a change.

None of these treatments promises a particular result. Your therapist will talk through what is realistic for your lashes and brows.

#### Who it suits

These treatments suit most adults who want to define their eyes without wearing make-up every day. A lash lift or LVL works best on natural lashes that have some length. Tints suit light or fine lashes and brows.

Please tell us if you have sensitive skin, allergies, an eye infection, or if you are pregnant or recently had eye surgery, as we may recommend waiting or choosing a different treatment. A sensitivity test is advised before tints, lifts and lamination; you can book the [sensitivity test](#prices) from the price list below. #### What to expect

1. **Before your treatment.** Your therapist will ask about your skin, your eyes and the look you would like, and will check your sensitivity test result. Please arrive without eye make-up if you can.
2. **During your treatment.** You lie back with your eyes closed while the products are applied and left to work. It should be comfortable and relaxing, and you can tell us at any point if anything feels uncomfortable.
3. **Afterwards.** Your lashes and brows will look defined straight away. Lifted lashes can look slightly more curled for a day or two before they settle.

Lash lifts and brow lamination typically last 6 to 8 weeks. Lash extensions need an infill every 2 to 3 weeks.

#### Aftercare

Keep your lashes and brows dry for 24 hours: avoid steam, saunas, swimming and heavy sweating. Avoid rubbing your eyes and sleep on your back if you can. Brush your lashes gently with a clean spoolie each day.

For extensions, avoid oil-based products around the eyes, as they can loosen the bond. Please do not pick or pull at extensions; come back to us for removal. If your eyes become red, sore or swollen, rinse with cool water and seek advice from a pharmacist or your GP.

### Questions and answers

**Do I need a sensitivity test?**

**What is the difference between a lash lift and LVL?**

Both lift and curl your natural lashes from the root so they look longer and more open, without extensions. The products and method differ slightly, so tell us the look you want and your therapist will recommend one.

**How often will I need an infill for lash extensions?**

Extensions fall out with your natural lash cycle, so an infill is typically needed every 2 to 3 weeks. The price list shows the classic and Russian infill times.

**Can I wear make-up afterwards?**

You can usually wear eye make-up the day after a lift, tint or lamination, but your therapist will give you advice for your own treatment. Eye make-up is better avoided straight after extensions or while your lashes are still settling.
