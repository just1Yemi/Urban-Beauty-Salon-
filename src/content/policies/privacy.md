## Who we are

The Urban Beauty Salon is the trading name of **{{ site.legalName }}**, a company registered in England and Wales, company number {{ site.companyNumber }}. Our registered office is {{ site.registeredOffice }}. Our salon is at {{ site.address.oneLine }}.

We are the "controller" of your personal information, which means we decide how and why it is used. To contact us about your information, email [{{ site.email.general }}](mailto:{{ site.email.general }}) or call [{{ site.phone.display }}](tel:{{ site.phone.tel }}).

We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

## What we collect and why

- **The enquiry form on our contact page.** We collect your name, email address, phone number (if you give it), what your message is about and your message. We use it to read and reply to you. Our legal reason is our legitimate interest in answering enquiries, or taking steps you ask for before a booking.
- **Online booking, through Phorest.** We collect your name, contact details, the appointments you book and any notes you add. We use them to make and manage your appointment, send confirmations and reminders, and keep your client record. Our legal reason is to carry out our contract with you.
- **Your client record at the salon.** This holds health information you give us for your safety, the treatments you have, patch test results and aftercare advice. We use it to treat you safely and keep a record of your care. Our legal reasons are our contract with you, and your explicit consent for health information.
- **Photos,** if we take any for your treatment record. We use them to record your treatment and check progress. Our legal reason is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
- **Website statistics,** which pages are visited and from which country or type of device, with no personal identifiers. We use them to understand what helps visitors and improve the site. Our legal reason is our legitimate interest in running the website well.

## Website statistics

We use **Plausible Analytics** to count visits. Plausible does not use cookies, does not collect personal information and does not track you across other websites. It records anonymous totals, such as page views, where visitors arrive from and a rough country. See our [cookies page](/cookies/) for more.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

- tell you what information we hold about you and give you a copy
- correct anything that is wrong
- delete your information, where we have no good reason to keep it
- limit or object to how we use it
- move your information to another provider, where this applies
- stop using your information where we rely on your consent

To use any of these rights, email [{{ site.email.general }}](mailto:{{ site.email.general }}). We aim to reply within one month.

## Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have used your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at [ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/) or on 0303 123 1113.
