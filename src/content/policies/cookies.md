## Our website

The Urban Beauty Salon website does not set cookies, so there is no cookie banner to accept. We do not use advertising or social media tracking.

Our statistics come from **Plausible Analytics**, which is designed to work without cookies and without collecting personal information. It counts page views and where visitors come from in anonymous totals. You can read how it works at [plausible.io/data-policy](https://plausible.io/data-policy).

## What the website stores in your browser

- **A small note that you have seen the logo animation,** kept in your browser's session storage until you close the tab. It stays on your device, is not sent to anyone, and is not a cookie.
- **Nothing else** for tracking, advertising or remembering you.

## Online booking with Phorest

When you press a Book now button, you use the booking panel run by **Phorest**, an independent company. Phorest's panel is loaded only when you point at, focus on or tap a Book now button, and it may show its own cookie notice and set its own cookies. Those cookies are controlled by Phorest, and its own policies apply.

## Maps and social links

Our map is a picture served from our own website, so it sets no cookies. The map, Instagram and Facebook links take you to other websites, which have their own cookie policies, only when you choose to follow them.

## How to control cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you do, Phorest's booking panel may not work properly. You can always book by phone on [{{ site.phone.display }}](tel:{{ site.phone.tel }}).
