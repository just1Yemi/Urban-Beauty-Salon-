## Our website

Every page of our website loads the **Meta Pixel**, a piece of code from Meta Platforms (the company behind Facebook and Instagram). It loads when you open a page and sets cookies in your browser.

The Meta Pixel tells Meta which pages of our website you visit. Meta uses this to measure how well our Facebook and Instagram adverts work, to show our adverts to people who have visited our website, and to find people similar to our visitors. If you are logged in to Facebook or Instagram, Meta can link your visit to your account. Meta also uses this information for its own purposes under its own policies: see [Meta's cookie policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policies/cookies/) and [privacy policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/).

Our statistics come from **Plausible Analytics**, which is designed to work without cookies and without collecting personal information. It counts page views and where visitors come from in anonymous totals. You can read how it works at [plausible.io/data-policy](https://plausible.io/data-policy).

## What the website stores in your browser

- **Meta Pixel cookies.** These include `_fbp`, set on our website for up to 90 days, which recognises your browser between visits, and Meta's own cookies, such as `fr`, if you use Facebook or Instagram. They are used for advertising and measurement.
- **A small note that you have seen the logo animation,** kept in your browser's session storage until you close the tab. It stays on your device, is not sent to anyone, and is not a cookie.

## Online booking with Phorest

When you press a Book now button, you use the booking panel run by **Phorest**, an independent company. Phorest's panel is loaded only when you point at, focus on or tap a Book now button, and it may show its own cookie notice and set its own cookies. Those cookies are controlled by Phorest, and its own policies apply.

## Maps and social links

Our map is a picture served from our own website, so it sets no cookies. The map, Instagram and Facebook links themselves take you to other websites, which have their own cookie policies, only when you choose to follow them.

## How to control cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, including blocking third-party or tracking cookies. You can also control the adverts Meta shows you, and the activity from other websites it uses, in your [Meta ad preferences](https://www.facebook.com/adpreferences/). If you block cookies, Phorest's booking panel may not work properly. You can always book by phone on [{{ site.phone.display }}](tel:{{ site.phone.tel }}).
