## What it is

Microneedling, sometimes called collagen induction therapy, uses a small handheld device fitted with very fine, sterile needles. The needles create thousands of tiny channels in the top layers of the skin. The skin responds as it would to any small injury, by starting its natural repair process, which includes making new collagen and elastin.

Because the change comes from the skin’s own repair, it builds gradually over the weeks after each session. The tiny channels also help serums applied during the treatment to reach deeper into the skin.

We offer three microneedling treatments, each tailored to a different concern:

- **Hydration and glow:** for skin that looks dull, tired or dehydrated.
- **Acne scars:** for softening the look of some types of acne scarring and uneven texture.
- **Pigmentation:** for uneven skin tone and patches of darker skin.

Your practitioner adjusts the needle depth and the serum used to suit your skin and the concern being treated.

## Who it suits

Microneedling can suit adults with uneven texture, enlarged-looking pores, fine lines, dullness, some acne scarring or uneven pigmentation. It is a good fit if you prefer a gradual change and are happy to commit to a course.

It is not suitable if you have active acne breakouts, a cold sore or skin infection in the area, a flare of eczema, psoriasis or rosacea, or a tendency to form raised (keloid) scars. You should also not have it if you are pregnant, have sunburn, or have taken isotretinoin for acne in the last six months. Some blood disorders and blood-thinning medicines may also mean it is not right for you. Your practitioner checks your medical history before treating you.

## What to expect

1. **Before your treatment.** Your practitioner assesses your skin and talks through your goals. You are typically asked to stop retinoids and exfoliating acids for three to five days beforehand and to avoid tanning.
2. **During your treatment.** Your skin is cleansed and a numbing cream is applied. The device is passed gently over the skin in sections, and a serum is applied to finish.
3. **Afterwards.** Your skin will look pink or red and may feel warm and tight, like mild sunburn. This typically settles within one to three days, and some light flaking may follow.
4. **Your course.** Sessions are typically four to six weeks apart, giving your skin time to repair between treatments.

## Aftercare

- Avoid make-up for 24 hours, and keep to a gentle cleanser and moisturiser for the next few days.
- Avoid retinoids, exfoliating acids and scrubs for at least three to five days, or as your practitioner advises.
- Wear SPF 30 or higher every day and avoid direct sun while your skin recovers.
- Avoid exercise, swimming pools, saunas and steam rooms for 24 to 48 hours.
- Let any flaking come away naturally; do not pick or peel the skin.
