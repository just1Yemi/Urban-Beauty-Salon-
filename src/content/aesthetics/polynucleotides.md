## What it is

Polynucleotides are purified fragments of DNA, typically taken from salmon or trout. Placed within the skin, they are designed to support its natural repair processes, and they are often chosen to improve skin quality, texture and fine lines, particularly in delicate areas such as under the eyes.

We offer several polynucleotide treatments: Plinest Eye and Plinest Fast, Vitaran Eye and Vitaran Face, and Rejuran Healer, Rejuran HB and Rejuran S. We also offer Newest, which combines polynucleotides with hyaluronic acid. Each is designed for slightly different areas or skin needs, and your practitioner will recommend the one that suits you.

Changes build gradually, so polynucleotides are usually given as a short course.

## Who it suits

Polynucleotides can suit adults who would like to improve skin quality, texture or fine lines, including the thin skin under the eyes. They are often chosen by people who want a gradual change in the skin itself rather than added volume.

Treatment is not suitable if you have a fish allergy. It is not usually suitable if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have an active skin infection in the area or have certain medical conditions. Please tell us about your health and any medicines when you book. Treatments are for adults aged 18 and over.

## What to expect

1. **Before your treatment.** You start with a consultation about your skin, your health, what you would like to improve, the options, the risks and the cost. Your skin is cleansed before treatment.
2. **During your treatment.** Your practitioner places small amounts of product with a fine needle or cannula. Most appointments take around 45 minutes, including time to talk.
3. **Afterwards.** Small raised bumps and some redness are common and typically settle within a day or two. Bruising is possible, especially under the eyes.

After a course, results typically last 6 to 12 months.

## Aftercare

Your practitioner will give you aftercare advice in writing. Typical advice for the first 24 to 48 hours includes:

- avoid touching or rubbing the treated areas
- avoid make-up on the area for the rest of the day
- avoid alcohol, strenuous exercise, saunas, steam rooms and sunbeds
- protect your skin from the sun with a high-factor sunscreen
