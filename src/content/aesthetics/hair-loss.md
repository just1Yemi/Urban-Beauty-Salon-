## What it is

Hair loss treatments aim to support the scalp and the hair follicles that are still active. They are a course of treatments, and changes take months to judge because hair grows slowly. We offer three options, and your practitioner helps you decide which, if any, is right for you.

### PRP (platelet-rich plasma)

A small sample of your blood is taken and spun in a machine called a centrifuge to concentrate the platelets. This platelet-rich plasma is then injected into the thinning areas of the scalp. Platelets contain growth factors involved in the body’s natural repair, and PRP is thought to support hair follicles. Research is still developing and results vary from person to person. PRP is available as a single session or as a course of three.

### Plinest Hair

Plinest Hair is a polynucleotide treatment. Polynucleotides are purified DNA fragments, derived from fish, that are injected into the scalp to support the condition of the skin around the hair follicles.

## Who it suits

These treatments can suit adults with early to moderate thinning, such as pattern hair loss in men and women, where hair follicles are still active. They are not designed for areas that are completely bald or for scarring types of hair loss.

Hair loss can be linked to iron levels, thyroid problems, hormones, stress or other health conditions. If your hair loss is sudden, patchy or rapid, or you have other symptoms, please see your GP first.

Treatment is not suitable if you are under 18, pregnant or breastfeeding, or have a scalp infection. PRP is not suitable if you have a blood or platelet disorder or take blood-thinning medicines, and Plinest Hair is not suitable if you have a fish allergy. Your practitioner goes through your medical history in full before treating you.

## What to expect

1. **Your consultation.** Your practitioner asks about your hair loss, health and medicines, looks closely at your scalp and talks through realistic expectations, the likely number of sessions and the cost.
2. **During your treatment.** Your scalp is cleansed. For PRP, a blood sample is taken and prepared during your appointment. The treatment is then given as a series of small injections across the thinning area.
3. **Afterwards.** Your scalp may feel tender, with small pinpoint marks or mild swelling. This typically settles within one to two days, and most people return to everyday activities straight away.
4. **Your course.** Sessions are typically four to six weeks apart, followed by maintenance sessions. Your practitioner reviews your progress over the following months.

## Aftercare

- Avoid washing your hair until the next day, then use a gentle shampoo.
- Avoid hair colouring, perming and other chemical treatments for 48 to 72 hours.
- Avoid strenuous exercise, saunas, steam rooms and swimming pools for 24 hours.
- Ask your practitioner whether to pause any medicines or supplements around your treatment, and do not stop a prescribed medicine without advice.
