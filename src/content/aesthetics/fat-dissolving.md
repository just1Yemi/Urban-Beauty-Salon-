## What it is

Fat dissolving uses a series of small injections into the layer of fat just under the skin. The solution is designed to break down fat cells in the treated area. Over the following weeks, your body clears them away naturally through its usual processes.

It is a gradual treatment. The change appears slowly over the weeks after each session, and a course is usually recommended.

We offer two options:

- **Face, one area:** for example, the area under the chin or along the jowls.
- **Body, one area:** for example, the tummy, love handles, bra line, inner thighs or upper arms.

## Who it suits

Fat dissolving can suit adults who are close to a stable, healthy weight but have a small area of fat that has not changed with diet and exercise. It is designed for soft fat you can gently pinch.

It is not a weight-loss treatment, and it is not designed for loose skin or cellulite. It is not suitable if you are under 18, pregnant or breastfeeding, or have an infection in the area. Some medical conditions, such as diabetes that is not well controlled, liver or kidney disease, autoimmune conditions or blood clotting disorders, and allergies to any of the ingredients may also mean it is not right for you. Your practitioner goes through your medical history before treating you.

## What to expect

1. **Before your treatment.** Your practitioner assesses the area, talks through your goals and explains the likely number of sessions, the side effects and the cost.
2. **During your treatment.** The skin is cleansed and the area is marked out, then the solution is injected in a grid of small injections. A warm, stinging or tingling feeling is common during treatment.
3. **Afterwards.** Swelling, tenderness, redness and bruising are common, and the area may feel numb or firm. This typically settles within three to seven days.
4. **Your course.** Sessions are typically four to six weeks apart. The change from each session develops gradually over the following four to eight weeks.

## Aftercare

- Avoid strenuous exercise, saunas and hot baths for 24 to 48 hours.
- Drink plenty of water and avoid alcohol for 24 hours.
- Wear loose, comfortable clothing over treated body areas.
