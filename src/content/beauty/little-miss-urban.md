## What it is

Little Miss Urban is a gentle pamper for children aged 4 to 12. Little Miss Nails is for fingers, and Little Miss Toes is for toes. Each appointment is short, calm and kept simple, so that it is a fun, relaxed experience for a child trying a salon treat.

**A parent or guardian must attend.** They stay with their child for the whole appointment.

## Who it suits

Little Miss Urban suits children aged 4 to 12 who would like painted nails or toes, whether for a birthday, a party or just as a treat. It is a gentle introduction to the salon because the appointment is short and your child stays with you.

If your child has sensitive skin, eczema, allergies or any nail or skin condition, please tell us before the appointment. We may suggest waiting until it has settled or choosing a different option. Anyone over 12 can choose from our regular nail menu instead.

## What to expect

1. **Before your treatment.** Your therapist will say hello, ask your child about the colour they like, and check their hands or feet. A parent or guardian stays with them.
2. **During your treatment.** Nails or toes are shaped and painted. Your child sits comfortably with you close by, and we go at their pace.
3. **Afterwards.** Polish takes a little while to dry fully, so bring open shoes or soft socks that will not smudge it. We are happy to answer any questions you have.

## Aftercare

Keep the polish dry for a short while, and try not to press or pick at it. Encourage your child not to bite their nails or peel the polish. Use a gentle, child-friendly hand cream if their skin feels dry.

If you notice redness, itching or soreness on the skin or around a nail, please contact the salon. Remove polish with a gentle remover, or ask us for advice.
