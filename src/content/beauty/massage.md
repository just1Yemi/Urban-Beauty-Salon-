## What it is

Massage uses touch and movement to ease tension in the muscles and help you unwind. Our menu has a choice of lengths and styles:

- **Back, head, and foot, leg and ankle massages** are short and focused, ideal when one area needs attention.
- **Deep tissue back massage** uses firmer pressure on tight muscles.
- **Hot stone back massage** uses warmed stones to add gentle heat as the therapist works.
- **A 20 minute face, chest and scalp massage** is a calming treatment for the upper body.
- **Full body massage** treats the whole body, with a longer option that includes the face and scalp.

Massage is a relaxation treatment. It is not a medical treatment, and we do not make claims about treating any condition.

## Who it suits

Massage suits most adults who would like to relax, ease everyday tension from desk work or busy days, or treat themselves. Deep tissue suits people who like firmer pressure; if you prefer a lighter touch, choose a standard massage.

Please tell your therapist if you are pregnant, have a medical condition, a recent injury, skin problems, high blood pressure, or take medication that thins the blood. In some cases we may suggest waiting, adjusting the massage or checking with your GP first. Massage is not suitable if you have a fever or contagious illness.

## What to expect

1. **Before your treatment.** Your therapist will ask about your health, any areas of tension or concern, and the pressure you prefer. They will explain how the massage will go and leave the room while you get ready.
2. **During your treatment.** You lie comfortably, covered with towels, while only the area being worked on is uncovered. Tell your therapist at any time if you would like more or less pressure or if you are too warm or cool.
3. **Afterwards.** You may feel relaxed or a little sleepy. Take your time getting up, and allow a few minutes before you drive.

## Aftercare

Drink a glass of water and take it easy for the rest of the day if you can. Gentle movement is fine, but avoid very hot baths and intense exercise straight away. Mild muscle tenderness after a deeper massage can happen and should ease within a day or two.

If you feel unwell, or any soreness lasts or worsens, contact the salon or speak to your GP.
