## What it is

Our men's menu keeps things simple. The **gentleman's manicure** and **gentleman's pedicure** tidy and shape the nails and look after the cuticles and skin of the hands or feet, leaving a clean, natural finish. [[TODO: what the gentleman's manicure and pedicure include, from the practitioner.]]

**Waxing for men** removes hair from the root, so skin stays smooth for longer than shaving. You can choose chest, back, or chest and back together. A men's brow wax is also available, listed on the waxing page.

## Who it suits

These treatments suit men who would like neat hands and feet, or smooth skin for sport, swimming, a holiday or a special occasion. No experience is needed, and your therapist will explain each step.

Please tell your therapist about any skin conditions, medication such as retinoids, sunburn or irritated skin before waxing. If you have a nail or skin infection, we may ask you to wait until it has cleared before your manicure or pedicure.

## What to expect

1. **Before your treatment.** Your therapist will ask about your skin, nails and health, and explain what will happen. For waxing, they will check your hair length and clean the area.
2. **During your treatment.** For a manicure or pedicure, you sit back while your nails are shaped and your cuticles and skin cared for. For waxing, wax is applied and removed in small sections. You may feel a brief sting, and we go at your pace.
3. **Afterwards.** Your nails will look clean and tidy. Waxed skin may look pink for a few hours.

## Aftercare

After a manicure or pedicure, use hand or foot cream each day and wear gloves for cleaning or manual work to keep nails and skin comfortable.

After waxing, avoid hot baths, saunas, gyms, sunbeds, sunbathing and tight clothing over the waxed area for 24 hours. Gently exfoliate after a day or two to help prevent ingrown hairs. Contact the salon if you notice persistent redness, swelling or signs of infection.
