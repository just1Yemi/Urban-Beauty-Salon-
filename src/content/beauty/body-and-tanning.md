## What it is

This page covers our treatments for the body and a spray tan.

**Spray tans** give you a tan without sun exposure. We offer the Sienna X spray tan for the full body and a half body option.

**Body exfoliation** removes dead skin cells from across the body, leaving the skin feeling smooth. It is a good preparation for a spray tan. ## Who it suits

Body treatments suit adults who would like to look after their skin and take time out. A spray tan suits anyone who prefers to avoid sunbeds and sun exposure.

Please tell us about any allergies, skin conditions, medication, pregnancy or recent surgery before your appointment. We may need to adjust or postpone some treatments, such as a wrap or CACI, if you have certain conditions or are pregnant. Spray tans are not a substitute for sun protection, so keep using SPF.

## What to expect

1. **Before your treatment.** Your therapist will ask about your health and skin, and explain the treatment. For a spray tan, you choose your shade and wear disposable underwear or swimwear.
2. **During your treatment.** Exfoliations and wraps are relaxing and unhurried. A spray tan takes only a few minutes, and the solution is applied evenly to the body.
3. **Afterwards.** Your skin will feel soft after a body treatment. A spray tan develops over several hours, so you will be given instructions on when you can wash.

## Aftercare

After a spray tan, wear loose, dark clothing and avoid water, sweat and moisturiser until your therapist says you can. Rinse with lukewarm water when told, pat dry rather than rub, and moisturise daily to help the tan fade evenly.

After exfoliation, wraps or other body treatments, drink water, moisturise, and avoid very hot baths and strong sun on the skin for the rest of the day. Contact the salon if your skin becomes red, sore or itchy.
