## Normal recovery

Every treatment has its own recovery, and your practitioner will tell you what to expect for yours. As a general guide:

- **Injectable aesthetics treatments:** some tenderness, swelling, redness, small bumps or bruising is common, and typically settles within a few days.
- **Facials, peels and microdermabrasion:** your skin may look pink or feel tight or dry for a day or two, and it is more sensitive to the sun. Use a gentle moisturiser and daily sun protection.
- **Lashes and brows:** keep them dry for 24 hours, avoid rubbing your eyes and avoid steam and saunas.
- **Waxing:** mild redness or small bumps are common for a day or so. Avoid heat, friction and strong sun on the area for 24 hours.
- **Nails and massage:** follow the care advice your practitioner gives you on the day.

Advice for each treatment is in the aftercare section of its page. See the list below.

## Warning signs

Contact us straight away, or call 111 or your GP if the salon is closed, if you notice any of the following after a treatment:

- pain that is severe or getting worse
- a pale, dusky or blotchy patch of skin
- signs of infection, such as heat, spreading redness, swelling that is increasing, pus, or a fever
- blistering, burning or a skin reaction that is spreading
- a rash, itching or swelling that could be an allergic reaction
- redness, soreness or swelling of the eyes after a lash or brow treatment, which needs advice from a pharmacist or your GP

If you have any change to your vision, call 999 or go to A&E first, then let us know.
